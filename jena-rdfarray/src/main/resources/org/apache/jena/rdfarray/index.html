rdf[]

RDF without the ceremony. A sequence is a term: (1 2 3) is one object, not a ladder of blank nodes. No rdf:first, no rdf:rest, no rdf:nil, no list-walking property paths or inference rules. The same data, queries and shapes, each written once in Turtle-array, SPARQL-array and ShEx-array.

Data (Turtle-array)

Shared by the three tabs. () is the empty sequence; sequences nest; a sequence may also be a subject.

Parse

The parser returns one triple per statement; a (…) object is a single seq term holding its elements.

Turtle-array

:alice :phones ("555-1234" "555-9876") .
1 triple. The object is a term with two elements.

RDF 1.1

:alice :phones _:l1 .
_:l1 rdf:first "555-1234" ; rdf:rest _:l2 .
_:l2 rdf:first "555-9876" ; rdf:rest rdf:nil .
5 triples, 2 blank nodes, 1 well-known IRI, and an invariant nobody checks.

Query

Validate

((t))[m, n] is a Collection shape expression: a sequence of m to n elements, each satisfying t. [m] means exactly m, [m, *] at least m, no brackets any length.

Node and shape may be <iri>, prefixed with the schema's prefixes, or relative to the server.
